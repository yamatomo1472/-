*Noun | /ˈtær.ɪf/ | Origin: from Arabic "ta'rifa," meaning notification or list of fees, which entered English via Italian and French in the 16th century*

**Definition:** A tax that a government places on goods imported from another country. Tariffs raise the price of imported products and are often used to protect local industries or as a tool in trade negotiations.
