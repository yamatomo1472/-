**Tariff**

*Noun | /ˈtær.ɪf/ | Origin: from Arabic "ta'rif," meaning notification or list of fees, which entered European languages through Spanish and Italian in the 16th century*

**Definition:** A tariff is a tax that a government puts on goods brought in from another country. It makes imported goods more expensive and is used to protect domestic industries or as a tool in trade negotiations.
